Hot Wings

Home Pan Chicken and Chick Peas

Description:

Hot wings one of my favorite junk foods is a SPICY
Chiken Wing.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Wings/Legs
  • Eggs
  • Oil
  • Flour
  • Sauce

Recipe:

  1. Prepare an egg wash.
  2. Dip wings and legs into egg wash
  3. Roll wings and legs into flour
  4. Put flour coated wings into heated pan with oil
  5. Remove once golden brown
  6. Toss in a bowl of your favorite sauce
  7. Enjoy
